The Offer

Free review copies available to readers — honest reviews appreciated but not required, and yours to keep either way.

How It Works

Simple, and no strings

1

Request a copy

Tell us your name, email, and whether you'd prefer print or ebook. Pick a title or just a genre you love.

2

We send it over

Your ebook link arrives by email; for print, we'll confirm your shipping details. The book is yours.

3

Read & enjoy

Take your time. There's nothing to send back and nothing owed — the copy is a gift for reading.

4

Review, if you'd like

If you enjoyed it, an honest review — good or otherwise — helps other readers find the books. Entirely optional.

Yours to keep

The copy is a gift for your reading and interest — never contingent on a review, and never returned.

Honest, either way

We ask only for your genuine opinion. A positive review isn't expected, and a critical one is just as welcome.

No pressure, ever

No deadline, no follow-up hounding. We may send one friendly note asking how you liked it — that's all.

One small thing to include
“I received a free copy of this book and am leaving an honest review.”

Adding this line keeps your review transparent and above-board — it simply tells readers you received a free copy. Please don't post the same wording everywhere; your own words matter most.
